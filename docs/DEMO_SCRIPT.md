# Five-to-seven minute hackathon demo

## 0:00–0:45 · Trust and scope

Open `/`. Introduce LegalBridge India as an attorney-assistance prototype aligned with SDG 16.3 and 10.3. Point to the two principles and the explicit synthetic/no-government/no-filing disclaimer.

Choose **Enter Demo Workspace**, fill the closed credentials, and sign in.

## 0:45–1:30 · Case and sources

Open **Nayak Property Papers Matter**. Show the status overview and recommended next action. Open Documents and identify the eight fictional source records. State that the browser retains metadata only and performs no upload or parsing.

## 1:30–2:20 · Deterministic multi-agent workflow

Start the workflow. Pause it and show that the active node stops. Resume, inspect a completed or active node, and point out fixed input, output, duration, and source IDs. Explain that refresh persistence is local and the sequence is deterministic.

For time, allow the workflow to continue while visiting precomputed modules or use a previously completed browser state.

## 2:20–3:15 · Traceable analysis

Open Timeline, filter to conflicts, expand the arrest memo and diary excerpts, and activate a source chip. Open Contradictions and compare the arrest, seizure, and witness accounts. Open Procedural Audit and stress “potential concern” and “requires attorney verification.”

Open Research, select one synthetic authority, and show applicability plus distinguishing facts. Point out the repeated “not verified legal corpus” warning.

## 3:15–4:20 · Strategy and ethics

Open Strategy. Show supported candidates and the attractive but unsupported intentional-fabrication allegation. Open Ethics and reject that argument. Read the explanation: sources do not support intent, the claim exceeds the evidence, it must not enter the motion, and verified evidence may be added later.

State clearly that Ethics Auditor approval is not attorney approval.

## 4:20–5:30 · Motion and Citation Firewall

Open Motion Studio. Show the editable draft, watermark, source inspector, and version history. Scroll to Citation Firewall:

- 9 legal citations
- 9 resolved source records
- 9 verified quotations
- 9 supported propositions
- 0 phantom citations
- 0 unsupported final claims
- 1 ethics rejection

Explain that these are synthetic interface metrics, not real legal verification. Note that export remains locked.

## 5:30–6:30 · Human approval and invalidation

Open Attorney Review. Enter a reviewer name, PIN `2026`, accept responsibility, and approve. Show version/hash binding and unlocked **Print or Save as PDF**. Open print preview, then cancel it; nothing is filed.

Return to Motion Studio, make a meaningful edit, and save. Show that approval is immediately invalidated and export locks again.

## 6:30–7:00 · Audit, observability, reset

Open Audit Log and point to approval, export, motion save, and invalidation events. Open Observability and distinguish fixed simulator metrics from production telemetry. Briefly demonstrate the mobile drawer. Finish in Settings by showing the safe reset confirmation.

Close with: “Autonomous until review, never autonomous at filing.”
